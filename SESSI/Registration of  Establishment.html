Consequent upon issuance of Notification u/s-1(3) of the Sindh Employees' Social

Security Act 2016, your establishment stands covered under Social Security Scheme w.e.f.  or from the date of starting business, whichever is later, vide Notification No.  issued by Government of Sindh. The secured workers and their dependents have become entitled to benefits in the manner provided u/s-36 to 46 of the Act. From the date of notification, you have became liable to pay to the Institution every month

Social Security contribution @ 6% of minimum wages in respect of every employee whether employed directly or through any other person (contractor). Further note that non-compliance of the provisions of Social Security Act, is an offence under Section-66 of the Act and the arrears of Social Security contribution including increase thereon are recoverable under the provisions of Land Revenue Act.
