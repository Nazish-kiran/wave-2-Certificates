  • Remove all excavated material from Jurisdiction area of SITE.
  • All Pipe/Cable/etc line must be in casing which should be minimum of 3 inch larger than the Pipe/Cable/etc. casing pipe should be HDPE or Ms. Pipe Position of laying of pipe will be decided by Concerned Engineer.

  • 12 Inch concrete material (1:2:3) on the road top after compaction layers (each layer size 24 inch).
  • 2 inch wearing on top layer.
  • (In Katcha cutting) All Pipe/Cable/ etc must be laying at least 3 feet deep at FRL.
